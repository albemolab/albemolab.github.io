The prototype

A research prototype that listens and reads

Detecting negative affect in Albanian conversations, from both text and voice.

The team built and tested a research prototype that reads a whole Albanian conversation and judges whether it carries negative affect. It looks at two things at once, what is said and how it sounds.

How it works

Speech is first transcribed with an automatic speech recogniser (Whisper). The transcript goes to text classifiers, from a simple word-frequency model (TF-IDF) to multilingual language models (XLM-RoBERTa and AlbaSocialXLM-R). In parallel, the audio goes to an acoustic model (WavLM). The system then combines the probabilities from both sides into a single decision.

Each conversation ends in one of three states, no alert, review or alert. In the experiments, an alert could also trigger an automatic email notification.

How it was tested

The evaluation used 860 Albanian audio recordings built from 150 distinct conversational scenarios, under controlled experimental conditions.

What it shows

The results support the technical feasibility of the approach. Before any real-world use, the system would still need external validation, privacy safeguards and human oversight.

Paper in preparation. A full description of the prototype and its evaluation is being written up by Ilma Lili and Emiranda Loka. This page will link to it once it is published.
