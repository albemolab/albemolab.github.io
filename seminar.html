Closing seminar

Research outcomes and what comes next

Friday 20 November 2026, 18:00 in Tirana and online.

The project closes with a seminar for professors and students. The team will present what it built and found, demonstrate the research prototype, and discuss lessons learned.

Programme

  • AlbEmo, the Albanian emotional speech corpus, and how it was built and validated.
  • Models for emotion recognition, including the cross-lingual study.
  • Publications and the main results of the project.
  • The research prototype, its architecture, and a live demonstration.
  • Discussion of lessons learned and future research.

Attending

The seminar is held in person in Tirana, with the online link available on request. To attend, write to Ana Ktona at ana.ktona@fshn.edu.al.
