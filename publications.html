Dataset

AlbEmo: a perceptually validated emotional speech corpus for Albanian (dataset)

Emiranda Loka, Ilma Lili, Alda Kika, Ana Ktona, Linda Mëniku and Alex Thomo. Zenodo, 2026. Licence CC BY 4.0. Audio, metadata and anonymised listening-test responses.

https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21638085

Code and supporting data

  • AlbEmo tools and scripts. The browser-based recording and listening-test tools, in Albanian and English, and the analysis scripts that reproduce every statistic and figure in the paper. github.com/thomouvic/albemo-public
  • Cross-lingual study code. Feature extraction, probing, transfer and speaker-removal experiments, MIT licence. github.com/thomouvic/ser-cross-ling-pub
  • Cross-lingual study data. Extracted model outputs for the public corpora, archived on OSF. doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/8VCH9
